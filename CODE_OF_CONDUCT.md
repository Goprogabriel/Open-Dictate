# Code of Conduct

Open Dictate is committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone who
participates, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while taking responsibility for impact.
- Respect privacy. Do not post credentials, personal information, or private dictated content.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, never on a person.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope and safety gracefully.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, doxxing, sustained disruption, and
publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, or other participation that violates this
policy and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. For sensitive conduct reports,
contact the maintainer privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile. Do not include a
conduct or security report in a public issue when that would expose a person or vulnerability.

Maintainers will review reports in good faith, limit disclosure to what is necessary, and consider
the safety and privacy of everyone affected.
